## 风格关键字速查

复制到 AI 工具的 negative prompt / 反向词里：

```
bright colors, neon, glow, heavy shadows, black outline, text, letters,
characters, people, figures, high saturation, dark background, pure black,
gothic, sci-fi, modern, futuristic, heavy border, thick lines, busy,
cluttered, high contrast, dramatic lighting, lens flare, particle explosion,
anime, cartoon, 3D render, photorealistic
```

风格正向关键词速查：
```
ethereal xianxia, jade aesthetic, ink wash painting, low saturation,
misty atmosphere, silk texture, cloud motifs, minimalist Chinese art,
celestial, airy composition, negative space, frosted jade, rice paper texture,
subtle gold accents, breathing room, elegant restraint
```
